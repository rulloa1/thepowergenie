Sunrun Downstream Conversion Reconciliation

Architectural guide for matching verified Sunrun partner reports with Power Genie campaigns.

1. What We Track On Power Genie

  • Inbound campaign source, medium, and campaign name.
  • Brand experience interaction (Power Genie vs Celebrity Solar vs Hip-Hop Solar).
  • CTA click timestamp and button placement (hero, mid_page, mobile_sticky, etc.).
  • Outbound navigation to authorized destination: https://sunrun.my.salesforce-sites.com/purl/?srep=/powergenie.

2. What Requires Sunrun Confirmation

  • Whether the homeowner completed the Sunrun form.
  • Whether Horace Lawrence performed a roof sizing consultation.
  • Whether a formal contract was executed and permitted.
  • Physical solar installation, PTO (Permission to Operate), and commission attribution.

3. Supported Reconciliation Methods

When Horace Lawrence or Sunrun provides periodic partner reports (CSV / Excel export), Power Genie campaign data can be reconciled by:
• Timestamp correlation: Matching outbound referral spikes with lead submission windows.
• Sub-brand alignment: Comparing inquiry notes referencing "Celebrity Solar" or "Hip-Hop Solar".
• Partner Lead IDs: Matching authorized referral identifiers if Sunrun confirms lead reference IDs.
