Questions people ask me

Fair questions.

Is it really $0 down?

With a Sunrun lease or power agreement there is little or nothing to pay upfront. You pay a monthly rate instead. That's why I say "pay for the power, not the panels."

Will I definitely save money?

Not guaranteed. It depends on your usage, roof, utility rates and location. That's the whole point of the quote: I'll put the numbers side by side so you can see for yourself.

What if something breaks?

Sunrun owns the system, so monitoring, repairs and insurance on it are covered for the life of the agreement.

Do I need the battery?

No, the battery is an optional add-on. It's worth it if outages are a problem where you live or you want backup power.

What if I sell my house?

The agreement can usually be transferred to the buyer. I'll walk you through how that works before you sign anything.

Will someone keep calling me?

No. You're dealing with me, Horace. If you say no thanks, that's the end of it.
