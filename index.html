Frequently Asked Questions

Where does my consultation request go?

When you click any solar CTA on Power Genie, you are directed directly to Sunrun's official secure Salesforce portal assigned to Horace Lawrence (srep=/powergenie). Sunrun's team receives your address and utility profile directly.

Does Power Genie collect my sensitive information?

No. In accordance with strict consumer privacy and compliance standards, Power Genie does not store your utility data or sensitive contact info in a secondary database. All submission data is handled directly by Sunrun's verified portal.

How does solar protect against power outages?

Standard solar systems without a battery shut down during a grid failure for utility worker safety. By adding an integrated home battery, your system automatically isolates from the grid and keeps key home appliances running uninterrupted.

Can I refer neighbors or family members?

Yes. Sunrun's official referral page allows you to submit a referral for friends, family, or community members interested in lowering their electric costs.
